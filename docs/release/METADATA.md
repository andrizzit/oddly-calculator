# App Store metadata draft

App name (working): **Oddly: A Curious Calculator**

Subtitle: **Serious math. Tiny nonsense.**

Primary category: Utilities. Secondary category: Productivity.

Keywords: `math,numbers,arithmetic,history,percent,offline,decimal,fun,calculator`

Promotional text:

> Meet the calculator with a tiny personality. Do the everyday math, save your answers, and discover eight curious little surprises along the way.

Description:

> Your numbers deserve a little personality.
>
> Oddly is a thoughtful everyday calculator with satisfying keys, useful history, and a companion who takes arithmetic seriously. Mostly.
>
> THE USEFUL PART
> • Everyday arithmetic with decimal precision and contextual percentages.
> • Square, square root, reciprocal, and pi within easy reach.
> • Recent calculations saved on your device. Recall or copy a result when you need it.
> • Repeat equals, correct a digit, and keep going.
>
> THE CURIOUS PART
> • Eight number discoveries to find and collect.
> • Hints when you'd like a nudge.
> • Calm, Playful, and Unhinged personalities. Same math. Different amounts of nonsense.
> • A warm interface, an optional dark theme, and optional haptic feedback.
>
> YOUR NUMBERS STAY YOURS
> No account. No advertising. No tracking. Works offline.
>
> Oddly behaves like a pocket calculator: binary operations run as you enter them, from left to right. Percentages work in context: 200 + 10% = 220, and 200 × 10% = 20.
>
> Built with native SwiftUI and released as open source under the MIT license.

Version: 1.0.0, build 1. Initial release notes: “A little room to think. A few numbers with personality.”
